Phoenixd is available now on [My LNbits](https://my.lnbits.com). This adds a ready to use, fully self-custodial Lightning wallet to LNbits on [my.lnbits.com](https://my.lnbits.com).

Phoenixd is a lightweight Lightning node that gives users full control of their funds while removing much of the complexity associated with running a full Lightning node.

Phoenixd combines self-custody with a streamlined experience thanks to automatic channel opening and liquidity management through channel splicing, meaning channels are resized on the fly, as needed with no user intervention.

You also benefit from excellent LN routing via ACINQ's Lightning Service Provider (LSP) improving the reliability of Lightning payments.

Phoenixd joins the growing range of wallet backends supported on My LNbits, giving users the flexibility to choose the Lightning backend to best suit their needs.

Ready to get started? Create an account on [my.lnbits.com](https://my.lnbits.com), create a new instance and select **Phoenixd** as your setup, and connect your own node in just a few clicks.
